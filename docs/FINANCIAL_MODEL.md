Revenue and most costs are in USD → the USD margin is FX-neutral. TRY costs inflate ~28%/yr, so the TRY “gain” from
depreciation is mostly illusory. Keep USD, convert monthly what you need, and bring earnings home (required for 89/13).

## If the estimates are wrong
- CTR and conversion at half the low end → base ≈ conservative → park at Revenue gate 1.
- Pro sells 3× (30 units) → +USD 1,000 net → B2B becomes the main plan.
- Break-even on cash: ~19 Home licences. Covering a TRY 4,000/month accountant needs ~10 Home sales per month.
