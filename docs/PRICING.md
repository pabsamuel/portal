# Pricing (planned — nothing is sold in v0.1.0)

Benchmarks: EarthCam “Webcams” app USD 4.99 one-time unlock; EarthCamTV Premium USD 19.99/yr plus a separate
commercial licence for lobbies/cafés/waiting rooms. Consumer willingness to pay in this niche is low.

| Tier | Price | Includes |
|---|---|---|
| Free | USD 0 | Both gesture modes, 3 destinations, compliant window mode for YouTube, small watermark |
| Home | **USD 9.99 one-time** (≈ TRY 299 local price) | All destination packs & themes, ambient mode, random portal |
| Streamer | **USD 14.99 one-time** | OBS mode, hotkey/HTTP trigger, transparent background, own-video portals |
| Pro / Event | **USD 49 / year** | Commercial/venue use, kiosk loop, custom colours & logo, priority support |

Rules
- Free tier must keep Windy content (Windy terms forbid putting it only in a paid tier).
- No consumer subscriptions in the MVP (subscription fatigue + low perceived value).
- Launch discount ≤ 30% for 7 days. 14-day no-questions refunds (a chargeback costs more: ~USD 15 dispute fee).
- TV store versions later mirror USD 9.99.
- Revisit after Revenue gate 1: if ≥ 30% of revenue is Streamer/Pro, raise Pro to USD 79/yr and test a USD 199 event licence.
