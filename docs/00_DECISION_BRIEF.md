# 00 — Decision Brief (read first)

**Verdict:** Build and test as a **time-boxed experiment**, not as a business yet.
- Window: **1 Oct – 23 Nov 2026** (demand gate). Cash cap: **USD 150 ≈ TRY 7,350** (planning rate USD/TRY 49.0).
- Time cap before the demand gate: **8 day-off sessions (~40 h)**. No paid ads before 23 Nov.
- Realistic 12-month net (estimate): **USD 0–3,000** in conservative/base cases. It will not replace factory income on its own.

## What changed on 30 Sep 2026
Samet decided the prototype shows **live YouTube cams inside the circular portal** (“dediğim gibi olsun”).
That is fine for a prototype, demos and personal use. It conflicts with YouTube’s API rules (“no overlays or frames
in front of any part of an embedded player”), so **before any paid/store release** one of these must be true:
1. `Compliant mode` is default for YouTube sources (portal animation → clean, unmodified rectangular player), or
2. the footage inside the portal is owned, public-domain, customer-supplied or licensed with a clause allowing effects.

## Hard limits
- Max CPA **USD 3.00** on the USD 9.99 Home licence (≈ USD 8 net per sale, estimate). One ad test ≤ USD 100, only after the demand gate.
- Refunds > 10% or any IP / ToS notice → pause sales within 24 h.
- Portfolio rule: this project is ACTIVE only while another venture is moved to MAINTENANCE (see PROJECT_MANAGEMENT_SYSTEM.md).

## Why not a full build now
- Licensing: YouTube RMF bans overlays; SkylineWebcams allows live embeds only for camera hosts; EarthCam needs written
  permission; Explore.org is non-commercial; Windy Professional costs €9,990/yr.
- Willingness to pay is low in the consumer live-cam niche (EarthCam app: USD 4.99 unlock; EarthCamTV: USD 19.99/yr).
- Owner capacity: 3 days off/week and 7+ active ventures.

## Where the real money probably is (hypothesis to test)
Streamers (OBS scene transition revealing their own content), party entertainers, escape rooms, venues and events —
paying for a **Pro/Event licence** to open portals onto *their own* footage. Consumer sales are the marketing engine.
